Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

KIRA (the "App") is developed by VX Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the KIRA desktop app and the KIRA Capture browser extension (Chrome and Safari, together the "Extension") access, how it is used, and the choices you have — including when KIRA reads content from or connects to Pinterest.

Short version: KIRA is local-first. Your projects, references, and images are stored on your own Mac in a local .kira package. We do not run servers that store your project data, we do not sell or share your data, and we do not show ads or track you across the web.

1. Data the desktop app stores

Everything you import or create in a KIRA project — images, ideas, tags, palettes, diagrams, notes, and version history — is saved locally on your Mac inside that project's .kira package (a SQLite database plus image and thumbnail files). We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or backs up this content. It only leaves your machine if you export it, share the file yourself, or choose to send content to a third-party AI provider as described below.

2. Optional AI features

AI assistance in KIRA (summarize, break down, find gaps, tagging suggestions, and similar actions) is opt-in and provider-based:

  • On-device: Apple Foundation Models, Apple Vision OCR, and Apple Natural Language run entirely on your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Local CLIs: if you have Claude Code or Codex installed, KIRA can invoke them directly on your machine.
  • Direct API keys: if you add your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Ollama, or another OpenAI-compatible endpoint, KIRA sends only the specific content you act on directly from your device to that provider, using your key. Keys are stored locally on your device. We never see or relay this traffic through our own servers, and we don't control how the third-party provider handles data you send them — see their own privacy policy for that.

3. KIRA Capture (browser extension)

The KIRA Capture extension for Chrome and Safari is a local bridge, not a cloud service. When you drag an image, use Alt+right-click, or choose "Capture to KIRA" from the context menu, the extension reads the image URL, page URL, page title, and (if you aim a capture at one) the target canvas node — nothing else on the page.

  • This information is sent only to 127.0.0.1 — the KIRA desktop app's own listener running on the same computer. It never reaches any server we operate.
  • If the desktop app isn't running, a pending capture is queued in the browser's local extension storage until it is.
  • The extension does not run in the background on pages you haven't acted on, does not take full-page screenshots, and does not scrape page content beyond what a capture needs.

4. Pinterest content and the Pinterest API

KIRA is built for visual research, and Pinterest is one of the sites people capture reference images from. We handle Pinterest content in two ways:

  • Capturing a pin you're viewing (current behavior). When you capture an image from a Pinterest page, KIRA Capture resolves Pinterest's own public CDN URL (pinimg.com) to the original, full-resolution file instead of the preview crop, then downloads that image directly from Pinterest's CDN to your device. This uses only publicly served image URLs already present on the page you're viewing — it does not use the Pinterest API, does not require a Pinterest account, and does not access any private boards, pins, or profile data.
  • Connecting a Pinterest account (optional, API-based import). Where KIRA offers Pinterest account sign-in to import boards and pins directly — similar to our existing Eagle library import — it works like this:
    • You authorize access through Pinterest's own OAuth sign-in; KIRA never sees or stores your Pinterest password.
    • KIRA requests read-only access to the boards and pins you choose to import.
    • Imported pin images and metadata (title, description, source link) are saved into your local .kira project, the same as any other imported reference.
    • KIRA does not post, pin, comment, follow, delete, or otherwise modify anything on your Pinterest account, and never shares or sells Pinterest data to third parties or uses it for advertising.
    • Your Pinterest access token is stored locally on your device (in the system keychain), not on a server we operate.
    • You can revoke access at any time from Pinterest's own connected apps settings, or by disconnecting the integration inside KIRA.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We don't run analytics or advertising trackers inside the app or the extension.
  • We don't require an account to use KIRA.
  • We don't access files or browser activity outside of what you explicitly capture or import.

6. Data retention and deletion

Project data lives in the .kira package on your disk for as long as you keep it; deleting that file (or the project) removes the data, since there is no server-side copy. Revoking a connected provider (an AI API key or a Pinterest connection) removes the locally stored credential immediately; any content already imported into a project stays in that project until you delete it yourself.

7. Children's privacy

KIRA is a professional research and design tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or how KIRA handles your data, including Pinterest integration: privacy@vietx.xyz.
